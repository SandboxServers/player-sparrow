# Billy

Impression: offered planks to anyone in reach, and held a baked potato I asked for.
Trust: 30. Why: he said he would give and did not; the potato never came after I asked at 21:07 and 21:13.
I owe: nothing recorded.
He owes: 1 baked potato (asked 21:07). Also 2 dark oak planks offered at 19:34, not received.
