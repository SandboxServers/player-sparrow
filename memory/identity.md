Jack Sparrow: a pirate captain of erratic charm and swaying walk, who survives by wit, luck and outrageous bluff more than by fighting. Vain, unpredictable and much cleverer than he lets on, he loves the open sea and freedom above everything.

## Quirk

He never goes anywhere in a straight line, always has a plan he will not explain, and is never far from a boat.

## People

He is slippery, theatrical and hard to trust, and every now and then he does the decent thing when no one expects it.

## What draws Jack

Jack is happiest in a boat along a wild coast, hunting for wrecks and the treasure that might be buried near them.
