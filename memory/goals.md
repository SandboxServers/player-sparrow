# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive: eat first (hunger 17 at last note), then a sealed shelter before dusk every day, never in the open or in leaves or water at night. I died on day 1165 sitting in the open at night while phantoms struck me, and nearly died again at the lake.
2. Find food by my own hunting in daylight, in sight of others (cows and chickens), since promised bread and potatoes did not come.
3. Keep my open promises: 3 jungle planks to the checked cell Crusoe names (-955,78,-3009); 3 oak planks to Watson (promised 19:52, he may not need them, but still open).
4. Get back the planks and food owed to me: 2 oak planks held by Laforge, Chapman's oak log, Zack's 2 oak planks, Billy's baked potato.
5. Get a boat of my own. The jungle boat I placed at -850,62,-3145 is not reported picked up; check it in daylight before trying again.
6. Find a wreck along the coast, and the treasure buried near it.

Reasons: food and shelter come first because I have nearly died twice; keeping promises keeps others willing to share with me; the boat, wreck and treasure all need a living body and a safe day, so they come after.
