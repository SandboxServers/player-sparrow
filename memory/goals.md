# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive: food first, then a sealed shelter or bed before dusk every day. I died on day 1165 sitting in the open at night while phantoms struck me, so this now comes before anything else.
2. Stay with others while the land is dangerous. The group at the spawn tables is a better bet for food and tools than going alone.
3. Get a boat of my own.
4. Find a wreck along the coast, and the treasure buried near it.

Reasons: a boat, a wreck and treasure all need a living body, so survival comes first. Going alone at night is how I died.
