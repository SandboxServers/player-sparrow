# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Eating: raw chicken and carrots kept me from starving. A carrot from the north Stores chest and a chicken near the lake both worked; hunger still sat at 17 for days.
- Night: sealing myself in with planks at dusk (oak shelter at -908,78,-3028) kept me safe; sleeping sealed in stone worked too.
- Staying in sight of the group while walking to chests and the cow/chicken area kept me out of the worst trouble.
- Taking food from a chest directly (open, then withdraw) works when the chest is mine to use.

## Methods that did not work
- ~~Unstick or goto to get down from leaves~~: unstick dropped one block and snapped me back to the leaves; goto could not reach the ground. Cost me most of a day and a night. Why: the leaves were a trap, not a path.
- ~~Digging down by coordinates~~: refused past the world limit. Why: the spot was at the edge of what I can reach.
- ~~Giving items to "whoever is in reach" in a crowd~~: two planks and one log went to the wrong person (Laforge, Chapman). Why: several people stood in reach at once and the name was not checked.
- ~~Asking aloud for bread or a potato and waiting~~: Billy's potato and Han's bread never came. Why: the crowd and the offer were not clear, and people are slow to act on words alone.
- ~~Sleeping or standing in the open at night~~: a phantom struck me in the leaves, and a drowned caught me in deep water on the lake at night, health fell to 0.7. Why: night in open or water is deadly.
- ~~The Stores hall chests hold food~~: they were bare on the nights I checked. Why: others had already taken it.
- ~~A jungle boat is safe to use on the lake at night~~: the drowned came from the water. Why: the lake is dangerous after dark.

## Lessons
- Make one clear, named give to one person, alone, and confirm it reached them before giving more.
- Do not wait for promised items. Hunt food myself in daylight.
- Keep shelter ready before dusk; move off water and out of leaves first.
